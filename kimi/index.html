A song is brief. Its echo does not have to be. Scroll through the small, slow alchemy inside Driftwood.

Live cross-section01 / Remember
  1. 01

    Keep listening like a person.

    Connect Spotify once. Driftwood notices tempo, repetition and the hours you keep—not your conversations, not your contacts, not your business.

    Input: your history, stored on the device
  2. 02

    The current learns your rhythm.

    A near-silent pump moves mineral-rich water over a tiny seed. A week of ambient might grow a patient branch. Three days of disco may take a stranger route.

    Translation: listening → flow → mineral
  3. 03

    Your year becomes an object.

    Calcium carbonate settles grain by grain. After twelve months, the pump switches itself off. What remains is not a chart. It is a small, odd souvenir of being here.

    Output: one coral, finished after one year

Details worth taking slowly

Less object.
More presence.

Nothing glows for your attention. Nothing asks to be optimized. Driftwood simply gets on with the business of becoming.

A / 01

No screen. Really.

The coral is the interface. There is no weekly report, no red badge and no “insights” tab hiding around the corner.

A / 02

Private by default.

Your history stays inside the device. It connects to fetch, never to upload. There is no account to leak and no microphone to wonder about.

A / 03

Grown, not printed.

Real mineral accretion makes every ridge. No two reefs share a silhouette, even if two people share every single playlist.

A / 04

It knows when to stop.

At day 365, growth ends and the water drains. Keep the finished coral dry forever, or return the cartridge for another year.
