The process

From sound to stone.

Three small steps. The first is probably something you’re already doing.

01 / INPUT

Press play.

Connect Spotify and listen as usual. Driftwood reads the little patterns in your listening history.

02 / PATTERN

Find a shape.

Tempo bends a branch. A new artist opens a fork. That song on repeat thickens a familiar stem.

03 / GROWTH

Give it time.

Each night, a fine layer of mineral settles into place. By morning, yesterday has a shape you can touch.

Object details

Small footprint.
Long memory.

01 / Scale

A place beside the speakers.

At 82 mm across, the base tucks between a coffee cup and the corner of a record sleeve.

02 / Matter

Mineral, through and through.

A replaceable mineral cartridge builds each coral form. Lift the glass to feel the ridges with a fingertip.

03 / Rhythm

Happy with a quiet week.

One USB-C cable keeps it growing. Take a break from music and your specimen rests until the next track.
