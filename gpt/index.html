Pre-order Driftwood How it grows

From track to tide.

A song lasts a few minutes. The shape it leaves takes weeks. Here’s what happens in between.

  1. 01

    Connect Spotify.

    Pair Driftwood once. It picks up the tracks you play and how often you come back to them.

  2. 02

    Keep listening.

    Repeat plays thicken a branch. A change in tempo sends a new one off at an angle.

  3. 03

    Watch it take shape.

    Each day the device lays down a thin mineral layer. Over a few weeks, those layers become a coral sculpture you can lift off the base.
