Log 02 · −8 m · The Descent

From play count
to polyp.

No ink, no filament, no resin tricks. Driftwood grows the way reefs do — mineral by mineral — just with better taste in music.

Sediment log01Step 01 / 04

Step 01 · Link

Sign in, once.

Link Spotify in the companion app. Driftwood reads your listening history — genres, hours, repeats, skips — never your microphone. Your shower singing stays between you and the tiles.

Step 02 · Seed

Cast your seed.

Drop a ceramic seed into the salt cell. Your first month of listening sets the base structure — the silhouette everything else will climb. Choose badly and you'll own a very honest monument to your podcast phase.

Step 03 · Accrete

The slow tide does the rest.

A whisper of current — five volts, gentler than a doorbell — pulls calcium from the seawater and lays it down where your music points. Ambient spreads wide and patient. Techno stacks fast and neon. Skips leave pale rings, like drought years in a tree.

Step 04 · Cohabit

Live beside it.

Top up the water monthly. Dust it occasionally. Otherwise, ignore it beautifully — it grows while you work, glows softly after dark, and never sends a push notification. The most interesting thing on your desk is now the one that says nothing.
