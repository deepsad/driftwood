Nº 01 — How it grows

Three quiet steps, repeated every night for as long as you keep listening.

  1. i.

    Listen

    Connect Spotify once. Around 3 a.m., while you and your songs are both asleep, Driftwood reads the day’s plays, skips and replays. Then it stops reading. It isn’t curious about anything else.

    Reads: plays · skips · replays · time of day

  2. ii.

    Translate

    Tempo becomes branching. Mood becomes color. A song you played eleven times becomes a thicker limb. The whole day folds into a single growth instruction, about as long as this sentence.

    120 bpm ≈ a fork every 4 mm

  3. iii.

    Grow

    A nozzle finer than an eyelash lays down 0.2 mm of mineral resin. You won’t see it happen. You’ll notice it one morning in March, the way you notice a friend’s hair got longer.

    ≈ 6 cm a year · makes no sound at all

Nº 04 — Questions

What people ask, usually while tapping the glass.

Is it real coral?

No. It’s a calcium-based mineral resin that grows, looks, and quietly clicks like coral when you tap the dome. Real reefs have enough on their plate.

What happens if I stop listening?

It rests. Nothing wilts, nothing dies. A quiet stretch leaves a smooth band, and growth picks up wherever you do.

What does it read, exactly?

Song, play count, skips, time of day. Not your messages, not your playlist names, not the one you made at 2 a.m. and never shared. Revoke access whenever you like; the reef stays yours.

Can two people grow one reef?

Yes, up to two accounts. Your branches braid together wherever your tastes overlap. Like most shared things, it gets interesting.

What happens when the dome is full?

Around year three, it tells you. Seal it, start a new dome, and keep the old one the way you’d keep a record.
